## 2023-10-25 - [Accessibility Issue] Missing aria-label on close buttons\n**Learning:** The application has numerous close buttons using `<IconClose />` that lack an `aria-label` attribute, making it difficult for screen readers to interpret their function. Since the `title` attribute isn't a reliable substitute for `aria-label`, these icon-only buttons need an explicit `aria-label`.\n**Action:** Add `aria-label={t("ui.close")}` to icon-only close buttons where they appear.
